# URLguard – store listing (English)

## Name
URLguard – phishing link protection

## Short description (≤132 characters)
Warns when a link only pretends to be your bank or a government site. Flags scam ads in search. Free, works locally.

## Full description
Scammers build pages that look exactly like your bank, the national e-health portal or the tax office – and they often appear at the very top of Google as sponsored ads. One careless login with Smart-ID or a password and the account is gone.

URLguard checks every link you open – from e-mail, SMS, social media or search – and warns you when it only pretends to be a known company or institution.

WHAT IT DOES
• Stops known phishing pages before they even load.
• Detects look-alikes: brand.com.something.xyz, brand-login.top, typos, look-alike characters (Cyrillic "а" instead of Latin "a").
• In search results (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo) shows a red ✕ next to scam ads and a green ✓ next to the official site. Scam results can optionally be hidden.
• Warns when a page asks for Smart-ID, eParaksts, a personal ID code or a password on a non-official address.
• Offers a "Go to the official site" button.

NOT ANNOYING
Nothing is shown on normal pages. Suspicious pages get a thin bar at the top. A full-page warning appears only for pages that are really dangerous.

PRIVACY FIRST
• All checks run on your device – the links you visit are never sent anywhere.
• No ads, no account, no tracking.
• Anonymous statistics (numbers only, e.g. "2 pages blocked today") – only if you opt in.
• Fully open source: https://github.com/Jazekss/URLguard

COMMUNITY
Seen a phishing page? Press "Report" – after review it is blocked for every URLguard user within hours. Wrong warning? Report that too.

Currently focused on Latvia (banks, government portals, telecoms, delivery services) plus global brands (Google, Microsoft, Apple, Meta, PayPal). The brand list keeps growing. Available in English, Latvian, Russian, Lithuanian and Estonian.

URLguard is a free, volunteer-run project.
